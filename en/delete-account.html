Delete account

Koto Home · Updated October 11, 2026

In the app

  1. Home → person icon in the top-right corner (My info)
  2. Delete account
  3. Confirm

By email

Send a message with the subject "Delete account" from your sign-up email address to kotoapp@outlook.com. We delete the account within 30 days.

Data deleted

The account and all its data: properties, tasks, logbook, costs, attachments and settings.

Retention

Data is removed from backups within 90 days. Usage statistics are deleted on request.
